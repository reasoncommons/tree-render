# Tree render options, 2026-11-11

Five style options on the v0.1 text, rendered on both fixtures (`v01-q2`, `v01-overview`) alongside the `coggle-tree` and `v01` starters. Styles: `skills/tree-render/render/styles/options-2026-11-11/`. Renders: `skills/tree-render/render/out/options-2026-11-11/`. Comparison page: `docs/design/options-page/index.html` (published as an Artifact; link in the session report). Engine asks: [2026-11-11-engine-asks.md](2026-11-11-engine-asks.md). Brief: [the revision note](../plans/2026-11-11-revision.md); measurements: [coggle-measurements.md](references/coggle-measurements.md).

## What was varied

Font step-down by depth, lead weight and continuation size and colour, attach mode (label riding the trunk vs. the branch ending at the text's side), branch width and taper per level, curve shape (late S-bend vs. mid-bend vs. bracket), palette saturation and whether text takes the branch hue, root treatment (grey box, soft box, plain text, plain text with a fan dot), and row spacing. The text is fixed.

### 1 coggle-faithful

Why: the measured Coggle grammar as a baseline, so everything else reads as a departure from it. What it shows: Coggle's labels sit on the last horizontal stretch of their trunk, which is the one place Coggle does what Rufus dislikes elsewhere (branch under or over text; see "Broader impacts" on the overview). The right-aligned L1 column also pushes the whole map right and leaves the root alone in a wide empty column. Verdict: keep as the reference, not the default.

### 2 coggle-refined

Why: Coggle's feel (thick tapered pastel ribbons, bracket fans, pointed tips, hue per branch carried into the text) with the branch ending at the lead's left edge at the first line's centre, so nothing runs under text, and with v0.1's typography (bold lead, lighter continuation, a real step-down). What it shows: the junction between lead and leaves reads clearly; the long horizontal run after a short lead ("The world races") is fine because it is Coggle's own idiom; the two-line lead ("Wisdom traditions / point towards restraint") works because the branch lands on the first line. Weak: a hairline hole at each L1 junction (engine ask), and the root sits in a tall empty column.

### 3 editorial

Why: least change from v0.1, which Rufus rated best; keep its type and only fix the drawing. What it shows: the continuation in muted grey under an ink lead is the strongest text hierarchy of the five, and the thin-but-real tapered branches already fix v0.1's main flaw. Weak: with five strong hues and ink text the picture is a chart more than a tree; the branches are still the quietest of the Coggle-feel options, and the "So…" branch is red only because red is palette slot 5.

### 4 tufte

Why: find the floor. Thin earth-toned lines, tight rows, steep type step, nothing else. What it shows: the overview fits in 998 px with every leaf on one line, which is the most efficient use of space here; the muted hues still tell branches apart. Weak: this is v0.1's drawing with colour, and Rufus has already said that reads as too thin and not beautiful; keep it as the dense-report variant rather than the default.

### 5 river

Why: the wildcard, from OneZoom rather than Coggle: taper about 0.5 per level, trunks that leave one point and sweep late into flat landings, big bold root. What it shows: taper by level is what makes the picture read as a tree at a glance; the bold lead in the deep hue over an ink continuation is the most legible hue-in-text treatment. Weak: louder than a report wants; the four hues on the overview are near "default chart" saturations and the trunks dominate the type.

## Recommendation

**Recommended: 2 coggle-refined.** It meets every item in Rufus's list: a colour per branch carried into the lead, Coggle's organic thickness, taper and late bend, a bold lead with a lighter continuation, a clear step-down (0.82), and the branch meets the text's side rather than running under it. On both fixtures it is calm enough for a report page and still reads as a tree from across the room. It is also the closest to the Coggle trees Rufus pointed at, minus the two things those trees do badly (labels on the trunk, crowded crossings). Its remaining faults are engine-side (junction cap) or layout-side (root in an empty column), not style-side.

**Runner-up: 5 river.** It is the most compelling image of the five and the one most likely to be shared: strong taper per level, a bold root, leads in the deep hue. It loses to refined on calm (saturated hues and 22 px trunks pull attention from the text) and on the overview tree, where four trunks of one width look uniform rather than organic. With a quieter palette and a per-child trunk width (engine ask) it could overtake refined for standalone web use.

Editorial is the safe fallback if Rufus wants v0.1's text hierarchy untouched; tufte is the dense-report variant; faithful is the reference.

## What is still weak (all options)

- The root sits alone in a tall left column on 4-5 branch trees; the layout centres it on the children's span. Engine ask: weighted root placement or a short root spine.
- Each L1 junction where several ribbons share a start point shows a hairline hole (refined, river at 2x). Engine ask: junction cap.
- The continuation always starts a new line; on the overview tree short leads ("Alignment") would read better run-in. Engine ask.
- Leaf text in the branch hue (faithful) costs contrast on the pastel greens and ambers; refined and river keep leaves in ink for that reason, which also makes the hue-in-text rule simple: hue for the lead only.
- No option yet handles a crossing branch; the text halo is the agreed fix if a dense tree needs it.
- The "So…" branch no longer stands out as the conclusion in palette mode; honouring `accent` inside palette mode would give it back.
