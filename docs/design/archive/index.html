2026-10

v0.1 mind maps

The first renders of the Wise AI Q2 tree as a mind map.

Verdict: "The best so far." The text is strong, the drawing is weak.

2026-11-07 to 2026-11-08

First design: Paper, Garden, Slate

Three themed card mock-ups plus an interactive prototype, with the branch as a shelf under the text.

Verdict: "Worse than v0.1." The branch under the text looks bad.

2026-11-07

Moodboard

Reference tools we looked at for taste. Four kept: Coggle default, a Coggle web3 taxonomy, Whimsical, and Markmap as an anti-example.

Verdict: Coggle is the bar ("nothing nearly as good as Coggle"). Whimsical is a reference only. Markmap is an anti-example.

2026-11-11

Six style options

Styles on the v0.1 Q2 text, each a different take on the mind map. River and refined Coggle were later tried on the whole Wise set.

Verdict: "These look bad... nothing nearly as good as Coggle."

2026-11-11 to 2026-11-12

coggle-exact

A dedicated engine reproducing Coggle's branches, colours, text and layout, compared with a Coggle original.

Verdict: "Much better... we now have a good coggle style."

2026-11-12

coggle-exact: tall layout

Collisions fixed; layout prefers tall over wide.

Verdict: "Prefer more vertical space and less width."

2026-11-12

Elegant: hairline, broadsheet, letterpress

Font and pigment variants on the coggle-exact engine.

Verdict: Only variations, "not the right direction". Hairline kept as coggle-thin. Book typography is not readable small; mono for some words does not work.

2026-11-12

Six diagram directions, round 1

Six different diagram forms on the Q3 tree.

Verdict: Dendrogram "works well but a bit dry"; spine "interesting, not really convinced"; outline "useful when we just have a doc, but not engaging or shareable"; braces "ok and quite clean but not as readable"; ink-branch "no, much worse than Coggle"; radial "does not work at all".

2026-11-12

coggle-thin

Thin-line Coggle, and a mono variant.

Verdict: Thin is a keeper. Root triangles liked after all for coggle-exact. Bold lead on its own line was reverted (dangling words). Mono dropped.
