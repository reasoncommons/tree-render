2026-11-12

Six diagram directions, round 1

Six different diagram forms on the Q3 tree.

Verdict: Dendrogram "works well but a bit dry"; spine "interesting, not really convinced"; outline "useful when we just have a doc, but not engaging or shareable"; braces "ok and quite clean but not as readable"; ink-branch "no, much worse than Coggle"; radial "does not work at all".

2026-11-12

coggle-thin

Thin-line Coggle, and a mono variant.

Verdict: Thin is a keeper. Root triangles liked after all for coggle-exact. Bold lead on its own line was reverted (dangling words). Mono dropped.

2026-11-12

Round 2 directions (2026-11-12)

Fable's second set after Rufus's round-1 verdicts. A: Coggle-style tapered ribbons in one slate hue, red for the conclusion, grey hairlines to points (v0.1 pattern); A-mono: charcoal ribbons. D: lanes (faint grey band per answer, no level-2 lines); D-hue: coloured. B: warmer dendrogram (curves then rounded elbows). C-hue: radial leads-only poster.

Fable's review: A > D > A-mono > B > C-hue > C; C not viable without hand-written short leads. Shown to Rufus: Coggle-thin, A, D, B.

Verdict: Rufus likes A (ribbon), B (dendrogram) and D (lanes); all three became CLI styles on 2026-11-13. Ribbon root to be centred on its dot, end dots removed.

2026-11-13

Five styles

The five CLI styles on the v0.1 Q2 text.

Verdict: Keep all five ("3–5 styles is fine"); root box padding scaled with 22 px root, triangles back on coggle-thin, end dots removed.

2026-11-13

Fonts and sizes, round 1 (phone views)

Four fonts on the Q3 tree, shown in a phone frame.

Verdict: Phone framing was not asked for and was rejected ("i didn't want a phone render"). Arimo not bad, Atkinson doesn't help, Merriweather terrible, Bricolage decent. Asked why no monospace.

2026-11-13

Layouts, fonts and sizes, round 2

Three layouts in Arimo, three more fonts, and two size options on the Q3 tree.

Verdict: Fan does not work; middle better but go further while keeping the curve shape. Arimo good, Bricolage really nice (default only on request; Arimo stays Coggle's default). Mono doesn't work at these sizes. Coggle's sizes 22/15.3/13 are best.

2026-11-13

Narrower middle run (Bricolage)

A narrower middle run in Bricolage on Q3 and the overview.

Verdict: "The middle just doesn't look right… the curvature isn't right." Work out the pattern of curvature, not per width.

2026-11-14

Coggle curvature rule

Coggle's own S drawn over a shorter shared run (at least 320 px), with a straight tail under the label. The rule is in docs/design/references/coggle-curvature.md. The first image is Fable's contact sheet.

Verdict: "This is great."

2026-11-14

Wisdom and AI: root and fuller questions

Ribbon in wise-ink with the root centred on its dot or outlined in a box, and fuller question text.

Verdict: Root centred on the dot, no box, is best; a box is liked too but should have square corners. Fuller questions (bold question, then a clarifying clause) are good. Published: same link, version 2.
