# Wisdom and AI

kind: question

- How should we approach AI development wisely?
  - **Can we make AI good?** Not just working well, but aligned with what is good; and what can wisdom traditions add?
  - **How fast should we go,** given the risk of catastrophe and the chance that alignment fails?
  - **How should we integrate AI** into our lives and societies, setting catastrophe aside?
  - **Could AI feel?** If it could be conscious or a moral patient, how should we relate to it?
