# Critique of trial v1 (2026-10-17)

Two subagents: a **cold reader** shown only the PNGs (phone glance ~10 s, slide ~30 s), and an **editorial designer + fact-checker** with the sources. Visuals: [trials/v1/q2-phone.png](trials/v1/q2-phone.png), [trials/v1/overview-slide.png](trials/v1/overview-slide.png).

## Q2 phone poster

**What worked.** The headline lands and the reader would stop scrolling; the type looks designed, not generated; bold-lead-then-muted-tail works; the cold reader recovered all four pillars and the 1 → 2 → 3 chain.

**Problems (merged, ranked).**

1. **Drift in the small text.** The So: band said actors *do* pull levers (source: *should*); levers lost qualifiers ("binding *external* checks", "agree red lines", "widen who decides"); "Individuals refuse to be carried" misattributed (source: individuals support pacing; *insiders* refuse to be carried). Pillar 4 lost "only modest help with mechanisms".
2. **The slogan is opaque without its definition.** Cold reader only decoded "speed of wisdom" as "slow down" from pillar 3. The policy (pace to what we can understand, verify, govern) never appears; the asymmetry line, the strongest argument, was styled as a subtitle and nearly skipped.
3. **Fake connective.** "AND 4" felt bolted on: pillar 4 is parallel, not part of the chain. "So" appeared twice (connective and band), muddying which is the conclusion.
4. **Internal labels and duplication.** "Q2" read as a business quarter; "Draft" appeared twice; "Wise AI" twice; no source link.
5. **Accent overuse** (kicker, stamp, numerals, connectives all oxblood); 30 px text is 10.8 px at 390 px, just under the 11 px floor; the black band (27% of height) competes with the headline.

## Area overview

**What worked.** Clean, readable; usable as an agenda/map slide; four hues backed by number and position; Q1 → Q2 and Q3's "setting catastrophe aside" relations were picked up.

**Problems.**

1. **No answer to the governing question**; columns alone are a table, not an argument.
2. **Relations not shown** beyond phrasing; "If it may not be…" is cryptic alone; dashed Q4 rule has no key.
3. **Drift:** Q4 dropped "may never know"; Q3 traditions line dropped friendship and stopping ("discipline" is a drafted word); Q2 traditions line's comma misleads.
4. **Internal jargon:** "Tree signed off · answers draft" means nothing to outsiders; the "Read each column down" footer is an instruction a layout shouldn't need.
5. **Slide legibility:** 24 px labels ≈ 12 pt; 30 px lines below the ~36 px (18 pt) body guideline. Q2's short answer leaves a hole.

## Lessons for the skill (folded into SKILL.md / PRINCIPLES.md)

- Run a **qualifier diff** for every compressed string; drift happens in the small text (levers, traditions lines), not the headline.
- **Connectives only where the source states the logic**; break the spine where a pillar is parallel.
- **Define a slogan headline** in one line; style the key reason as argument ("Because …"), not as a subtitle.
- **Write for a stranger**: no node ids or internal status on public formats; status once.
- **Floors per format**: phone 32 px on a 1080-wide canvas; slide body ≥ 32–36 px, labels ≥ 28 px on 1920×1080.
- **Overviews need an answer** (drafted and flagged if the source has none) and visible relations.
