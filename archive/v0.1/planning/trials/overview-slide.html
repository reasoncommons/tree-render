How should we approach the development of AI wisely, and what do wisdom traditions offer?

We can only partly make AI good, so go at the speed of wisdom, keep AI a servant, and stay humble about its inner life.

1 · Alignment
Can we make AI good?
Partly, and not yet in a way anyone can check.
Traditions offer
The target and a model of formation, not a technical fix.
2 · Risk and pace
If we can’t be sure, how fast should we go?
Go at the speed of wisdom: slow the frontier, speed up safety.
Traditions offer
A clear direction towards restraint and the will to act; little on mechanisms.
3 · Broader impacts
Setting catastrophe aside, how should we live with AI?
AI’s deepest effect is on our capacity to choose well; keep it a servant.
Traditions offer
Practices of consumption and stopping, standards of speech and friendship, community.
4 · Consciousness
Could AI feel? (a minor strand)
We do not know and may never; relate with humility, low-cost care and clear honesty.
Traditions offer
A better question: is there feeling that can suffer?
