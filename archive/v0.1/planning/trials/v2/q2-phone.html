Wise AI briefing · Risk and pace
How fast should we develop AI?

Go at the speed of wisdom.

Pace AI to the speed at which we can understand, verify and govern it.

Because catastrophe would be permanent, while most of what delay costs can be recovered.

1
The risks are real and contested, and the uncertainty itself calls for caution.
yet
2
The world races because it is caught in a collective action trap.
even so
3
We should slow and steer, we partly can, and far more people should decide.
4
Wisdom traditions point towards restraint and are strongest on will and process; they offer only modest help on mechanisms.
So every actor (labs, states, communities, individuals) should push AI towards the speed of wisdom, each with its own lever.
